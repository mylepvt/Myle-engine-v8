============================================================
 MYLE APP — STEP BY STEP GUIDE (WhatsApp format)
 Niyam: har step ka text bhejo, phir uske neeche [SS] wala
 screenshot laga do. Text seedha WhatsApp me paste ho jaata hai.
============================================================


────────────────────────────
 PART A — CALL KAISE KAREIN & LOG HO
────────────────────────────

*📞 STEP 1 — Leads page kholo*
App kholo → menu se *"Leads / Work"* par jao.
Yahan har lead ek _card_ ki tarah dikhti hai.

[📸 SS 1 — Leads list page, jisme lead cards dikh rahe ho]


*📞 STEP 2 — Lead ka card dhundo*
Jis customer ko call karni hai uska card dhundo.
Card pe naam, phone number aur ek _clock timer_ 🕐 dikhega.

[📸 SS 2 — Ek single lead card — naam, phone, timer clearly dikhe]


*📞 STEP 3 — Green Dial button dabao*
Card ke neeche *green 📞 (Dial)* button dabao.
➡️ Phone dialer khulega *aur* system call apne aap log kar lega.

[📸 SS 3 — Card ka green Dial button highlight karke]


*📞 STEP 4 — Call status set karo*
Call ke baad card pe *📞 Call status ▾* dropdown kholo aur chuno:
• Called - No Answer  (uthaya nahi)
• Called - Interested  (interested hai)
• Called - Not Interested  (mana kar diya)
• Called - Follow Up  (baad me baat)
• Called - Busy  (line busy)
• Call Back  (usne wapas call bola)

[📸 SS 4 — Call status dropdown khula hua, options dikhte hue]


*📞 STEP 5 — Lead status update karo*
Agar lead aage badhi (jaise video bhejna hai) to
*● Lead status ▾* dropdown se stage update karo.

[📸 SS 5 — Lead status dropdown khula hua]

✅ _Bas! Call log ho gayi. Phone na uthe tab bhi Call status zaroor set karo._


────────────────────────────
 PART B — STALE LEAD (THANDI LEAD) MAT HONE DO
────────────────────────────

*🕐 STEP 1 — Timer dekho*
Har card pe ek clock timer hai.
• Green = time hai, sab theek
• Red / "SLA" = time khatam, lead thandi pad rahi hai ⚠️

[📸 SS 6 — Ek card jisme RED/overdue timer dikh raha ho]


*🕐 STEP 2 — Lead garam rakho*
Time khatam hone se pehle koi action lo:
📞 Call  /  💬 WhatsApp  /  *… (3 dots) "Follow-up +24h"*
"Follow-up +24h" timer ko 24 ghante aage badha deta hai.

[📸 SS 7 — 3 dots (…) Follow-up button highlight karke]


*🕐 STEP 3 — Agar archive ho gayi*
24 ghante tak koi action na ho to lead _archive_ ho jaati hai.
Neeche *"View archived leads"* link dabao → lead dhundo → *Restore* karo.

[📸 SS 8 — "View archived leads" link + archived list]

⚠️ _Archive = delete nahi. Restore se wapas aa jaati hai._


────────────────────────────
 PART C — LEADER KO UPDATE / ESCALATE
────────────────────────────

*🎖️ Team member ke liye:*
Koi dikkat ho (lead handle nahi ho rahi, verification, payment proof)
to leader ko *WhatsApp* pe turant batao.
_11:00 baje verification escalations chalti hain — pending task turant clear karwao._

[📸 SS 9 — (optional) WhatsApp pe leader ko update bhejte hue]


*🎖️ Leader ke liye — WhatsApp commands:*
App ke WhatsApp number pe sirf ye word bhejo, jawab turant aata hai:
• `status`  → aaj kitni report submit / missing
• `missing` → kin members ne report nahi di
• `top`     → is hafte ke top performers

[📸 SS 10 — WhatsApp chat jisme "status" bhej ke reply aaya ho]


*🎖️ Lead kisi aur ko dena (sirf leader/admin):*
Card pe *⚙ (Reassign)* button dabao → lead top performer ko transfer.

[📸 SS 11 — Card ka Reassign (⚙) button highlight karke]


────────────────────────────
 🎯 3 SABSE ZAROORI BAATEIN
────────────────────────────
1️⃣ *Har call log karo* — Dial + Call status set karo
2️⃣ *Lead garam rakho* — timer khatam hone se pehle action
3️⃣ *Report time pe* — raat 11:59 se pehle submit (+20 points)

_Confusion ho to leader se pucho — lead thandi mat hone do._ 😊
